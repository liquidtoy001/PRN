# Print-note length diagnostic

For roughly 100 content-bearing slides, fewer than 10 substantive A4 pages triggers an
over-compression review. Approximately one note page per ten effective content units is a
warning baseline, not a minimum or ceiling. Dense mathematics may need substantially more;
verified duplicate frames or sparse visual slides may justify fewer. Record the reason after
checking coverage. Exclude covers, contents, audits and blank pages. Do not inflate typography,
margins or repeated prose to pass. Adapt this diagnostic for other paper sizes and explicitly
condensed requests; Markdown-only output is judged by taught K IDs rather than screen length.
